## What I believe

I believe we're tied down by invisible orthodoxies. The laws of physics are the only limit. That's why I don't want to fix my identity — nature has no boundaries. Explore as much as you can. Try different things. Follow your curiosity. It's one way to get closer to the truth and experience the beauty of life.
